The

The best fight the best. That’s the only way the claim gets verified. Every fighter on this list has called himself a champion. Some of them are. But the fights that would settle it are still unmade — the ones the public deserves, the historical record requires, and somebody keeps finding a reason to avoid. The fans haven’t gotten to see. The accounting is live. This page holds the record until the bell rings.
