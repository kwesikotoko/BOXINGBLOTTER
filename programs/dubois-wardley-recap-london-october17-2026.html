Game Story
⚑ Blotter Flag · Narrative Spine
The Blotter’s lean was Dubois by stoppage. It arrived in the second round, and it arrived fast.
Dubois Got There First, and Stayed There
Dubois stopped Wardley in round 11 in May to take the WBO title. The rematch at the O2 took less than six minutes.

Dubois came out to land the first big shot and did. A jab opened the door, a straight right walked through it, and Wardley was down inside the first twenty seconds. He beat the count and got to his feet, but he was hit hard again before the bell, and the round told the champion what the next one could be.

Wardley spent the opening minute of the second round trying to get his legs and his range back. Dubois did not give him the time. A clean right landed, a barrage followed, and the fight was close to over before anyone in the arena had settled back into their seat.

Referee Steve Gray stood back as the shots piled up, and the towel came in from Wardley’s corner at 2:21 of round two. Dubois called Wardley brave and said it was his time and his era. Wardley said there is no shame in losing to the best, and that he will keep taking hard fights.

Daniel Dubois · Fabio Wardley
Dubois Now Picks the Next Name

Filip Hrgovic, the IBF champion, was at ringside and called for a unification in March. Promoter Frank Warren suggested Hrgovic may have to face other contenders first, and Hrgovic is also reported to owe a defense to mandatory challenger Frank Sanchez. Dubois said he wants Hrgovic and the IBF belt. No date is set. Agit Kabayel, Sanchez and Usyk are all names in the mix.

Wardley, 31, has now lost to Dubois twice and says he will keep taking tough fights. His next fight will show how quickly he can rebuild.

Louie O’Doherty · Michael Gomez Jr.
A Draw Nobody Seems to Own

O’Doherty keeps both titles and an unbeaten record of 12-0-1, while Gomez, who started fast and had the only card in his favor, leaves without the belts. A rematch would be an easy sell. None has been announced.

Bakhodir Jalolov
18-0 and Still Waiting on a Big Name

Jalolov passed a ten-round test and moved to 18-0 with 15 knockouts. The next step is an opponent who can test him over distance.

Prospect Watch
Five Fighters Worth Following

Adam Olaniyan is 5-0 with five stoppages, Nelson Birchall is 13-0 with 10, and Fawaz Aborode, Aaron Noriega and Manny Safari all won by stoppage in their first few pro outings. Zayn Ahmed and Leighton Birchall are both unbeaten as well.
