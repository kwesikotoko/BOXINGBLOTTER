Matthew
Saad Muhammad

"Miracle Matthew"
Light Heavyweight · 39–16–3, 29 KOs · 1954–2014
IBHOF Class of 1998 · Philadelphia, Pennsylvania

Down on the cards. Cut over both eyes. That's when he found another gear that shouldn't have been there. He was found abandoned on a Philadelphia sidewalk as a child — and grew up to pick his own name, win a title in his rival's home town, and pay for the search that finally answered where he came from. Miracle Matthew was never a puncher's name. It was a survivor's.
